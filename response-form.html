Alumni prototype feedback

Complete this individually after trying the prototype. 1 = Very Poor; 10 = Perfect. There are no right answers. Leave a criterion blank if you cannot assess it and explain below.

Enter only your participant code, not your name. Your answers stay in this page until you download them. Nothing is sent automatically. Refreshing loses unsaved answers.

1. Interfaces

How easy were the search, demo sign-up and sign-in screens to use? Think about whether you understood what to enter and which button to select.

2. Regional Structure

How clearly was each page organised? Think about whether the menus, headings, information and buttons were where you expected to find them.

3. Colour scheme

How well did the colours work? Think about whether text stood out against the background and colours were used consistently across pages.

4. ICON Design

How easy were the icons (small symbols or pictures) to understand? Think about whether you could tell what they meant or what would happen if you selected them.

5. Data Model

How well did the example information support the tasks? Think about the fictional sign-in details, search results and profile information. Rate what you could see and use; you do not need to judge the technical system behind it.

6. Visibility of system status

How clearly did the website show what was happening after an action? Think about whether you knew which step you were on, whether something had worked, or whether you needed to correct an error.

7. Aesthetic and minimalist design

How simple and uncluttered did the pages feel? Think about whether you could focus on what you needed without being distracted by extra information or decoration.

8. Content

How clear and useful were the words and information? Think about whether the headings, instructions and service descriptions helped you understand what was available and what to do next.

9. Usability

How easy was it to complete the tasks? Think about the effort involved, moving between pages, and whether you had to remember information from an earlier screen.

10. User Experience

How would you rate your overall experience of using the prototype? Think about how comfortable, confident or frustrated you felt while using it.

Tell us about your experience

One or two sentences for each question is enough. These sentence starters are optional: use your own experience, and leave a question blank if you prefer.

Sentence starter: “The ___ helped me because ___.” You can also write “Nothing in particular.”

Sentence starter: “When I tried to ___, I was unsure about ___.” If nothing was difficult, you can say so.

Sentence starter: “I would change ___ so that ___.” You can also write “I would not change anything.”

Sentence starter: “I could not rate ___ because I did not try it / did not understand what it meant.” If you could answer them all, write “I could answer all the rating questions.”

Do not include names, passwords or other personal details in comments.
